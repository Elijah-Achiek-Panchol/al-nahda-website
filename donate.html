Support Our Mission

Al-Nahda Innovative Academy depends on support from individuals and partners to continue offering training, scholarships, and teacher development opportunities for refugee youth, learners, and educators. Donations help ensure that education and training remain accessible even when resources are limited.

Ways to Invest in Our Community

Sustain Daily Education

Provide one-time or monthly giving options to sustain ongoing student learning tracks, baseline workshop facilities, and structural operations inside Ain Shams.

Scholarship Sponsorship

Directly sponsor dedicated youth paths through our formal Scholarship Program, offsetting tuition fees, exams, and localized equipment configurations.

Hardware & Material Gifts

We welcome physical donations of vital functional assets including laptops, desktop hardware, development kits, electronics units, and verified learning materials.

Make a Direct Impact

  • Direct tuition support and program scholarships for refugee learners.
  • Computers, essential electronic lab kits, and physical software utilities.
  • Curriculum optimization maps and professional teacher pipeline resources.
  • Safe, robust, and technologically equipped physical classroom spaces inside Cairo.

Support a learner or teacher today and help open a path to education, skills, and opportunity.

Commitment to Transparency

Al-Nahda Innovative Academy strives to use all resources responsibly and transparently, focusing on student and teacher benefit and sustainable educational impact.
