Our Mission & Vision

Al-Nahda Innovative Academy is dedicated to providing high-quality, practical STEM education, digital literacy, and professional pipelines to empower youth, refugees, and educators in Cairo, Egypt.

Meet Our Dedicated Team

Our team brings together educators, engineers, volunteers, and coordinators who are committed to helping students and teachers succeed and supporting their journey through education.
